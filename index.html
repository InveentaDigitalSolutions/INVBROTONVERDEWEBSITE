Broton Verde is a family foliage nursery in the hills above Lake Yojoa, Honduras. We grow stock plants under shade net and sell what we cut from them: unrooted and rooted cuttings for growers in North America and Europe.

A cutting that waits a day too long arrives soft, and a soft cutting roots badly or not at all. So the whole nursery runs to the flight: stock beds pruned on a calendar, cutting at first light, boxes closed the same day.

You order by line, grade and quantity. Every piece is graded on the table before it is counted, the count on the lid is the count inside, and the invoice says the same.

WEEK 0

Planted.

Rooted stock goes into a fresh, drained bed under 70–80% shade net.

WEEK 2

Established.

Drip irrigation and fertigation on schedule; the bed fills in.

WEEK 6

First cut.

The bed joins the rotation. Pruned to keep it producing, never stripped.

EVERY WEEK AFTER

Graded on the table, counted into the box, on the Wednesday flight.

THE CATALOGUE

Five groups.Thirty-two lines.

Pothos is the volume programme and Sansevieria the second. Satin Pothos, Heartleaf Philodendron and Dieffenbachia complete the list. Every line is sold unrooted or rooted, in seven grades, with availability posted every Friday.

THE NURSERY

Santa Cruz de Yojoa,564 metres up.

Warm days, cool nights and the humidity that rolls off Lake Yojoa give foliage an even, year-round growing climate. Shade net does the rest: filtered light, no scorch, and cuttings that stay turgid from the bed to the box.

FROM BED TO BOX

From the bedto your bench in days.

01

Stock beds, on rotation

Stock plants grow in ground beds and raised baskets, planted and pruned on a calendar so there is always a bed at the right stage to cut from.

02

Water, fed and filtered

Drip lines run off a filtered manifold with fertigation on a fixed programme. The cutting you receive was grown on a schedule, not on rain.

03

Cut, graded, counted

Cutting starts at first light. Each piece is graded on the table, then counted into the box by line and grade.

04

Papered and flown

Phytosanitary certificate, packing list and invoice travel with every consignment. Boxes close the day they are cut and leave San Pedro Sula on the Wednesday flight.

LOGISTICS

Two routes,every week.

Cuttings do not wait well, so we fly and never sail. Two departures a week, one north and one east, and door-to-door delivery on request.

  • North AmericaSan Pedro Sula → Miami, Houston, Los Angeles
  • EuropeSan Pedro Sula → Amsterdam, Frankfurt
