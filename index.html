A cutting that spends a day too long in a box arrives soft, and a soft cutting roots badly or not at all. So the nursery is built around the flight.

Stock beds are pruned on a calendar. Cutting starts at first light. Every piece is graded against its published size, counted into the box by hand, and on a plane out of San Pedro Sula the same week.

You order by variety, size and box. The count on the lid is the count inside, and the invoice says the same.

WHAT WE CUT

Three kinds of cutting.One count per box.

URC · UNROOTED CUTTING

Tip and stem cuttings, ready to stick.

The bulk of the catalogue: Aglaonema, Begonia, Dieffenbachia, Fittonia, Hedera, Peperomia and more. Cut from stock beds pruned on rotation, so every cutting has a fresh node and a clean base.

  • LINES66 varieties
  • PER BOX500 – 12,000, by line
  • SIZEPublished per variety
  • CUT-OFFWeekly
L&E · LEAF AND EYE

One leaf, one node. The vining lines.

Pothos, Philodendron, Scindapsus and Monstera are cut leaf-and-eye: a single mature leaf with its node and aerial-root initial, sized on a grid so a 6-inch cutting is a 6-inch cutting.

  • LINES24 varieties
  • PER BOX1,000 – 8,000, by line
  • SIZE4″ – 10″, by line
  • CUT-OFFWeekly
LEAF · LEAF CUTTING

Single leaves for the Peperomias.

Watermelon, Red Beauty and Raindrop are cut as leaves with a short petiole, packed flat and dense. Slow, steady lines that reward a patient bench.

  • LINES3 varieties
  • PER BOX2,400 – 11,400
  • SIZE3″ – 4″
  • ROOTEDProgrammes on request

FROM BED TO BOX

Cut on Monday.On your bench by Wednesday.

01

Stock beds, on rotation

Ground beds and post-line baskets are planted and pruned on a calendar, so there is always a bed at the right stage to cut from.

02

Water, fed and filtered

Drip lines run off a filtered manifold with fertigation on a schedule. The cutting you receive was grown on a known programme, not on rain.

03

Cut, sized, counted

Cutting starts at first light. Each piece is checked against the published size, then counted into the box by variety.

04

Papered and flown

Phytosanitary certificate, packing list and invoice travel with every consignment. Out of San Pedro Sula by air, the same week.

LOGISTICS

Two routes,every week.

Cuttings do not wait well. We fly, we never sail, and the week is built around two departures: one north, one east.

  • North AmericaSan Pedro Sula → Miami, Houston, Los Angeles
  • EuropeSan Pedro Sula → Amsterdam, Frankfurt
